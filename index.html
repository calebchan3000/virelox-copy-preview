The System

You Record. We Run This Loop.

Four stages, every week: research decides what gets made, and results feed the next brief.

01

Research & Script

We decide what will work before anything is made: the questions your audience is already asking, and the educational gaps nobody covers well. Then we strategize the script with you. Starting from zero? Channel setup is on us.

02

You on Camera

You record one session a week, about an hour, talking points ready. Founder, operator, educator, or named company talent. Named presenters, always.

03

Edit & Package

Editors cut for retention. Titles and thumbnails are finished so the right people click and stay, because the video actually teaches something.

04

Publish on YouTube

We publish to YouTube, package each video for discovery, and manage the channel week over week. Performance feeds next week's brief. YouTube only: no other platforms, no paid ads.
