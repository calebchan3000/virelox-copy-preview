How we work

Simple by design.

You record about once a week; we handle everything else and grow you through organic YouTube reach.

  1. 01

    About an Hour a Week

    One recording session a week. That's the whole lift. We grow your personal brand on YouTube from there, in your voice, through organic reach. You stay on camera; we run the rest.

  2. 02

    Named Presenters Only

    You (the founder, operator, educator, or named expert) front every video. Real people the audience can actually get to know.

  3. 03

    Your Voice, Not Ours

    Educational long-form in your company's unique voice. We research and package it; the point of view, stories, and expertise stay yours, never generic creator-agency fluff.

  4. 04

    Organic Reach, Not Ads

    Growth comes from YouTube discovery: people finding and watching work that helps them. No paid media. Views that compound because the videos are worth watching.
